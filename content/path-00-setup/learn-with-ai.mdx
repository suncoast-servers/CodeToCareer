## Outcome

You have a personal rule for what you will and will not accept from an assistant.

## Theory

Ask for explanations, tests, and small edits. Do not ship code you cannot explain. Do not paste secrets or customer data into a chat. "The model said it works" is not a test run.

## Do this

Break `hello.py` on purpose. Ask for a diagnosis before a fix. Write `notes/ai-policy.md` in your own words: what was wrong, and two things you will never paste into a tool.
