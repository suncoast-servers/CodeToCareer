## Outcome

You know the order: Path 0, a track, labs on your machine, then a certificate checklist.

## Theory

Videos are scattered. This site is the sequence. Paths are folders of lessons. Labs are files you run. AI can speed you up, and you still run and explain the work.

## Do this

1. Star the [GitHub repo](https://github.com/suncoast-servers/codetocareer).
2. Create a learning repo and paste in [`templates/portfolio-readme.md`](https://github.com/suncoast-servers/codetocareer/blob/main/templates/portfolio-readme.md).
3. Write three sentences: why you are learning, hours per week, and the date you want to finish SQL.
