## Outcome

You can list emails, count customers, and insert a row without creating a duplicate email.

## Theory

`SELECT` chooses columns. `FROM` chooses the table. `COUNT(*)` counts rows. `INSERT` adds a row. `ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING` means a second insert of the same email is skipped because `email` is unique.
