## Outcome

`greet("Ada")` returns `Hello, Ada`.

## Theory

Arguments are the inputs. An f-string (`f"Hello, {name}"`) drops the variable into the text. The quotes around the whole string stay. The quotes around `{name}` would be a mistake if you want the value, not the word name.
