## Outcome

You can use `git diff` to decide what to keep.

## Theory

A model edits files. `git diff` shows those edits. If a line is a surprise, you do not understand the change yet. Ask what it does, or delete it and try a smaller request.

## Do this

1. Change `hello.py` with an assistant, or by hand.
2. Run `git diff`.
3. In `notes/ai-policy.md`, paste two lines you kept and one sentence on why each is correct.
