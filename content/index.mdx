## How a lesson works

1. A few minutes of theory.
2. Something you type and run.
3. A checkpoint, plus a lab check on your machine.

Nothing you need in order to learn is behind a paywall. YouTube and disclosed tool links are how the project can stay free. Details: [Support](/support/).
