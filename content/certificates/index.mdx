Certificates mean you finished the published drills. They are not a paid PDF.

## Earn one

1. Finish the track lessons and lab checks.
2. Keep a public portfolio repo.
3. Run `node certificates/issue.mjs <track-id> "Your Name"` on a machine that holds the issuer key.
4. Open [Verify](/certificates/verify/?id=) with your id.

Track ids live in `certificates/catalog.json` (`ctc-coder-foundations`, `ctc-ai-assisted-dev`, `ctc-data-engineer`).

## LinkedIn

Add the certificate by hand, or start from this prefilled form and replace the id:

[Add Coder Foundations](https://www.linkedin.com/profile/add?startTask=CERTIFICATION_NAME&name=Coder%20Foundations&organizationName=Code%20to%20Career&issueYear=2026&issueMonth=11&certUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fcodetocareer.org%2Fcertificates%2Fverify%2F&certId=ctc-coder-foundations)

Put your verify URL in the certificate URL field: `https://codetocareer.org/certificates/verify/?id=YOUR_ID`

## Upwork and Fiverr

Those sites do not import certificates. Link your verify URL and your GitHub portfolio in the profile text.
