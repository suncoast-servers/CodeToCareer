Code to Career certificates mean you **finished published requirements** in the open repo—not a paid PDF mill.

## How to earn one

1. Finish a [career track](/tracks/) (see `certificates/catalog.json` on GitHub).
2. Keep **public proof** (GitHub portfolio, lab screenshots, capstone).
3. Request verification (Phase 1: self-attest + public checklist; Phase 2: signed badge).

## LinkedIn

Use **Add profile section → Licenses & certifications**, or the prefilled link for your track (generate from catalog):

Example (Data Engineer):

[Add Data Engineer certificate on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/profile/add?startTask=CERTIFICATION_NAME&name=Data%20Engineer&organizationName=Code%20to%20Career&issueYear=2026&issueMonth=3&certUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fcodetocareer.org%2Fcertificates%2F&certId=ctc-data-engineer)

Replace `certId` with your issued ID when verification launches.

## Upwork & Fiverr

There is **no official certificate import**. Do this instead:

- Link `https://codetocareer.org` and your **GitHub portfolio** in profile.
- Paste verification URL in project descriptions.
- Use certificates as **talking points** in proposals—not as a platform badge.
